## Entry

> Don't tell Ana she's overreacting in the school car park on the last day of term.
> Those five words ended a ten-year friendship in a minute, and listening that afternoon would have kept her beside you at your dad's funeral.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or to `$ENTRY_OUTPUT` when set, or to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` / `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when one of those is set). The entry is exactly two lines and 223 characters including the trailing newline, within the 1–280 character limit.
